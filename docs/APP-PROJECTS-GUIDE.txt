MOBILE APP PROJECTS

1. Open /admin and sign in. Select Mobile apps immediately after Work projects.
2. Click Add mobile app or open an existing app.
3. Select its app category, enter the app name and one-line card description, and upload its image/logo. This image appears on both the gallery card and detail hero.
4. Add the introduction, then click Add content section. Add sections with optional headings, paragraphs and full-width images. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Use Move up to reorder sections.
5. Enter Google Play and Apple App Store HTTPS links. Leave unavailable links empty; their buttons will be hidden.
6. Use the CMS header language buttons to enter reviewed Arabic content separately. Store links and images are shared across languages.
7. Save draft, then Publish. All published mobile projects appear under All; category buttons filter them. See more opens the app detail page.

Desktop uses three columns (nine apps form three rows); smaller screens use two or one column. More than nine apps continue in additional rows.
Existing project IDs keep page links stable when you rename an app. Deleting and publishing an app removes its detail page.
