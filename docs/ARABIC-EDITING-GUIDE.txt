YelloStack: English and Arabic content editing

1. Open /admin and sign in. The editor starts in English, independently of the public website language.
2. Use English / Arabic in the top header to select the content language.
3. In Arabic mode, edit translated text in the visual editor or Content lists & settings. English source text is preserved.
4. Save draft, review the preview, then Publish. Preview forms cannot send enquiries.
5. Adding, deleting and reordering items applies to both language versions. Links, images, categories, contact numbers and numeric company figures are shared.
6. New English wording needs a reviewed Arabic entry: switch to Arabic and enter the translation. This does not use an automatic translation API.
7. Blog articles display their Arabic version when Arabic is selected. In Blog articles, use the header language buttons to edit Arabic titles, introductions, sections and captions separately from English.
8. Digital marketing is available in Services, the main services menu and the homepage service list. Its page is /digital-marketing-agency.

Public Arabic wording uses Dammam and the corrected Arabic spelling of Al-Husaini requested by the team.
