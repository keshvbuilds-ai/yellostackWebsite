YELLOSTACK - CMS EMAIL SETUP GUIDE
Updated: 3 November 2026

1. OPEN EMAIL SETTINGS IN CMS

After the new website release is deployed:
1) Open https://yellostack.com/admin and sign in.
2) Select General in the CMS menu.
3) Under Contact & office, enter the Sales email. This is the inbox that receives contact form submissions, Easter egg claims and test emails.
4) Click Save draft, then Publish to activate the Sales email.
5) In General, scroll below Easter egg offer to Private email settings (SMTP).
   SMTP is a section within General, not a separate tab.
6) Enter the sending mailbox settings using the Google or Zoho steps below.
7) Click Test connection & send email. This saves your SMTP settings and sends a real test message to the published Sales email.
8) Check that inbox and its spam/junk folder for Test Connection Email.

Save SMTP settings saves without sending a test. SMTP changes apply immediately: no Publish or server restart is required after saving them.

2. WHAT EACH FIELD MEANS

SMTP host: Your email provider's outgoing mail server address.
Port: Choose STARTTLS - 587 for the examples below. TLS - 465 is also supported.
SMTP username: The full email address of the mailbox sending notifications.
Sender email: Use the same address as the SMTP username for the simplest setup.
SMTP password / app password: A password generated by the email provider for this website. This is not the CMS login password.
Sales email: The recipient. It can be a different mailbox from the sender.

The password is masked (hidden) and is never displayed back after saving. Leave it blank when changing other SMTP settings to keep the saved password. Paste a new app password to replace it.

3. GOOGLE / GMAIL / GOOGLE WORKSPACE

A) Sign in to the Google account that will SEND website notifications.
B) Open Google Account > Security and enable 2-Step Verification.
C) Open https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
D) Create an app password named YelloStack Website.
E) Copy the generated password into the CMS SMTP password field. Paste without the display spaces.

Enter in CMS:
SMTP host: smtp.gmail.com
Port: STARTTLS - 587
SMTP username: Your full Google mailbox email
Sender email: The same Google mailbox email
SMTP password: The generated Google app password

Click Test connection & send email and check the Sales email inbox.

If App Passwords is unavailable, your Google Workspace administrator or account security policy may restrict it. Ask your administrator; do not use your normal Google password instead. Changing your Google account password can revoke app passwords, requiring a new one.

Official instructions:
https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833
https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/imap/imap-smtp

4. ZOHO MAIL

A) Sign in to the Zoho mailbox that will SEND website notifications.
B) In Zoho Mail, open Settings > Mail Accounts and select your mailbox. Locate Server Configuration Details / outgoing SMTP settings.
C) Copy the exact SMTP server shown for your account. It depends on your plan and data centre.
   Common examples: smtp.zoho.com (personal/free organisation accounts) or smtppro.zoho.com (paid organisation accounts). Other regions can use different suffixes. The value in your own account is the one to use.
D) Open Zoho Accounts > Security > App passwords.
E) Choose Generate New Password, name it YelloStack Website, and generate it. Enable MFA if your account requires it; contact your administrator if the option is restricted.
F) Copy the generated password into CMS without spaces. Zoho displays it only once.

Enter in CMS:
SMTP host: The outgoing server copied from your Zoho account
Port: STARTTLS - 587
SMTP username: Your full Zoho-hosted mailbox email
Sender email: The same mailbox email
SMTP password: The generated app password

Click Test connection & send email and check the Sales email inbox.

Official instructions:
https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/zoho-smtp.html
https://www.zoho.com/mail/help/adminconsole/two-factor-authentication.html

5. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER SETUP

Contact form -> Sales email -> Subject: Contact Form Submission
Easter egg claim -> Sales email -> Subject: Easter Egg Found!!!
Connection test -> Sales email -> Subject: Test Connection Email

Visitor notifications include their submitted name, email, phone and message, submission time and enquiry ID. Easter egg notifications also include the discount, coupon and offer terms. Emails have a YelloStack logo footer and plain-text alternative. Replying to a visitor notification addresses the visitor.

Visitors do not receive an automatic offer or confirmation email. They see the website's thank-you message. Their submissions stay saved in CMS Inbox if email delivery fails.

6. CHECKING DELIVERY / TROUBLESHOOTING

- A successful test means the SMTP provider accepted the email. Confirm receipt in the recipient inbox or spam folder.
- Only one test is allowed per minute.
- If authentication fails: verify the full mailbox username and generated app password.
- If the sender is rejected: use the same mailbox for SMTP username and Sender email, or a provider-approved alias.
- If connection fails: verify host, port and outbound SMTP connectivity from the VPS.
- If mail goes to the wrong recipient: update Sales email, Save draft and Publish, then test again.
- For failed visitor notifications: open CMS Inbox, check Email delivery, and use Retry notification after correcting settings. Existing enquiries keep the Sales email captured when they were submitted.
- If the SMTP section is missing: the latest release needs to be deployed; refresh the CMS after deployment.

7. PRIVATE STORAGE / BACKUPS

SMTP settings are encrypted on the VPS and are excluded from published website content. Do not put credentials in GitHub or screenshots shared with others.

Back up the entire /var/lib/yellostack directory securely, including store.json and smtp.key. The key is needed to decrypt the saved settings. Keep both files private. Encryption does not protect against someone who can read both files.
